# Theme: rejected candidates

Palettes tried before the macOS-native theme in `.streamlit/config.toml`, and what ruled each out — a contrast figure for three of them; for indigo / zinc, that it re-adds what `7daa4f2` removed. Read this before proposing a different palette, so none is re-derived; the theme's live rules and the contrast pairs it must clear are under **Theme** in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Rejected alternatives, so they are not re-derived.** A two-line `stock-blue` (Streamlit's own `#1c83e1` / `#0068c9` as primary and nothing else) fixes the collision at the smallest pin surface and won the maintenance lens, but keeps Streamlit's navy-tinted greys, no field edges and stock light mode's 3.3:1 error red. A DAW-inspired near-black stage with 3:1 borders (`meterbridge`) is the only way to get the white label to 4.5, at the price of a desaturated teal that collapses to grey under protanopia and borders that dominate the layout. A VS Code palette put the dropzone's **Upload** button at 1.02:1 against its well. Re-adding the dropped indigo / zinc (the bundled `minimal` template) would re-add exactly what `7daa4f2` removed. Every candidate was rendered through the real app in both modes and in both the result and the error state before choosing; the harness is the screenshot harness in `docs/screenshots.md`
