## The button right edges

The "original stale claim" the paragraph below refers to is the `BUTTON_WIDTH` bullet's — that the two buttons shared a right *edge* rather than a width; the bullet in `CLAUDE.md` still says an earlier version of it did. When the pairing changes again, append the new state here and bump the count in the `CLAUDE.md` pointer, rather than rewriting the paragraph.

Note the pairing has now changed three times, which is why the measurement is restated rather than reasoned about.
